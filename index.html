Our Educational Philosophy

Where Play Meets Learning

At Little Minds, we believe early childhood is the most crucial developmental stage. Our holistic play-based curriculum integrates Montessori and Reggio Emilia principles to inspire self-discovery, social bonding, and emotional resilience.

  • Low student-to-teacher ratio (1:5 max)
  • Organic nutritional meals and snacks included
  • Spacious outdoor play areas and organic garden
